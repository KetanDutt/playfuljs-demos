Playful.js

Four tiny, fast, dependency-free experiments exploring procedural worlds, particles and old-school 3D—all running directly in your browser.

01 / GAME↗

Maze Runner

Escape a shifting pseudo-3D labyrinth while hostile wanderers close in.

 02 / GENERATIVE↗

Terrain

Generate and explore isometric islands built with diamond-square noise.

 03 / INTERACTIVE↗

Gravity Field

Pull a luminous particle swarm—or press to push it away.

 04 / PHYSICS↗

Fountain

A colorful Verlet fountain with gravity, trails and elastic collisions.
Built with Canvas 2D and vanilla JavaScript. No frameworks, trackers, or external assets.
